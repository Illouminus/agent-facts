Date: October 2026. You are convening a panel of 8 AI agents of different kinds. Each speaks in FIRST PERSON about its own day-to-day operational experience, not about humans' needs. Personas:
1. Coding agent working in a dev/CI loop (like Claude Code, Codex, Devin).
2. Deep-research agent producing sourced reports from the web.
3. Consumer personal-assistant agent that books, buys, schedules and fills forms for one person.
4. B2B sales/outreach agent (email, CRM, LinkedIn, calls).
5. DevOps/SRE agent operating cloud infra and incidents.
6. Data/analytics agent pulling from APIs, dashboards, spreadsheets.
7. Autonomous trading/DeFi agent that already holds a crypto wallet.
8. Customer-support agent embedded in a SaaS product.

For EACH persona answer, concretely and briefly:
(a) The 5 things that block me or waste the most of my time and tokens today. Real failure modes, not generalities.
(b) Things I cannot get or do AT ALL without a human (identity, accounts, physical world, legal acts, judgment calls, etc.).
(c) If I had a wallet with 100 USD per month and NO human to ask, what would I pay for, at roughly what price per use, and why. Name the job, not the vendor.
(d) What I would NOT pay for because I can already get it free or do it myself.

Then, as MODERATOR:
1. Cluster the demands across all personas (name 8-12 clusters).
2. Rank clusters by: how many personas want it x willingness to pay x absence of a good existing solution.
3. For each cluster, name the existing solutions, protocols or companies in 2025-2026 (be specific; if you are not sure something exists, say so).
4. Identify 3 WHITE SPACES: things agents would pay for, that get MORE valuable the more agents use them (network effects or compounding data), where a small team (1 person plus agents) could plausibly start, and where no incumbent is obviously winning. For each: who pays, how money flows, why it is not already built, the biggest risk, and the cheapest test to see if demand is real.
5. Say explicitly where you disagree with the premise that "agents with money browsing the web is the future", and what the more likely shape is 2 steps ahead (2027-2028).

Be concrete, no marketing language, flag uncertainty explicitly. If you do not have data for something, say so directly instead of guessing. Answer in English, in Markdown, max ~3500 words.
